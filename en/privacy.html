Privacy policy

Last updated August 2, 2026

What we collect

The GOAT has no sign-up, login, or forms. We don't collect your name, email, or any personally identifiable data for you to play.

Where your save lives

Your career progress is saved exclusively in your browser's local storage (localStorage), on your own device. It is never sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data erases the save.

Metrics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure visits in aggregate. This tool doesn't use cookies, doesn't build a user profile, and doesn't track you across sites.

Advertising

The game may show ads from partners (such as Google AdSense) to stay free. Ad networks may use their own cookies to serve and measure ads; when that goes live, this page will be updated with the details and consent controls required under the LGPD (Brazilian data protection law) and GDPR.

Your rights

Since we don't keep your personal data on a server, there's no database to request access to or deletion from — clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything. Questions: alvarorumpel@gmail.com.
