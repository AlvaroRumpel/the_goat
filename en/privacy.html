Privacy policy

Last updated August 13, 2026

What we collect

The GOAT has no sign-up, login, or forms. We don't collect your name, email, or any personally identifiable data for you to play.

Where your save lives

Your career progress is saved exclusively in your browser's local storage (localStorage), on your own device. It is never sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data erases the save.

Metrics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure visits in aggregate. This tool doesn't use cookies, doesn't build a user profile, and doesn't track you across sites.

Advertising and cookies

The GOAT shows Google AdSense ads to stay free. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve, measure and (where you consent) personalize ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. This includes the DoubleClick cookie, which Google uses for frequency capping and ad performance measurement.

Where the law requires consent (such as in the European Union and the United Kingdom), only non-personalized ads are shown until you consent; non-personalized ads still use cookies for frequency capping, aggregate reporting and fraud prevention, but do not use your browsing history for targeting.

You can control personalization:

  • Turn off Google personalized ads at adssettings.google.com;
  • Opt out of third-party vendor cookies at optout.aboutads.info (US) or youronlinechoices.com (Europe);
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Details on how Google handles data on partner sites: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Ad networks process this data on the legal basis of consent or legitimate interest, as required by the GDPR and the LGPD (Brazilian data protection law).

Your rights (GDPR and LGPD)

We keep no personal data of yours on our own servers. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes the save and everything else stored on your device. For data processed by ad networks (such as Google), you can exercise access, correction and deletion rights directly with the vendor through the links in the section above. Questions about this policy: alvarorumpel@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If how the game handles data changes (for example, new ad partners or new measurement tools), this page will be updated and the date at the top will reflect the revision.
