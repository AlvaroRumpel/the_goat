How to play The GOAT

The GOAT is a free basketball career game that runs right in your browser — no sign-up, no install, in English and Portuguese. You build a player by stealing attributes from the NBA's greatest legends, live an entire career season by season, and get a final verdict ranging from "Playground Baller" to THE GOAT.

1. The attribute draft

Everything starts with the attribute draft. Each round the game draws a legend and you steal one attribute from them — the shot of one of history's greatest scorers, the court vision of a genius point guard, the frame of a dominant big man. Every steal has a price: you also inherit a malus, a penalty derived from that legend's biggest weakness. Stealing from a slow giant can cost you speed; stealing from a frail shooter can cost you strength. Once per draft you can reroll if the legend on the clock doesn't fit your build.

The trick is putting together a coherent build: a pure scorer, a cerebral playmaker, a relentless defender — or a balanced do-it-all. The weaknesses you accept in the draft follow you your whole career.

2. The three game modes

  • Career — the default mode: you see each legend's values before choosing what to steal.
  • GOAT Mode — blind steal: the legend's values and the price stay hidden until the draft ends. For anyone who wants to test their instinct.
  • Quick Mode — the season runs on its own, no game screens: you only make the career decisions (offers, trades, retirement). A full career in a few minutes.

3. The season, game by game

After the draft comes the real NBA draft: teams make offers with different plans — contend for a title right away, rebuild patiently, or shine in a big market. Your choice sets the context for the season.

The calendar has 82 games and is alive: the whole league — the other 29 teams — is simulated alongside you, with standings, an awards race, and trades. Some games in the season are key games: rivalry classics, direct position battles, special games. In those, you step onto the court: the play-by-play runs on the clock and the game pauses at 2 to 5 decision moments, where you choose between the safe play, the bold play, or the reckless play. On the final possession, an 8-second timer tightens the choice (you can turn it off on the start screen).

Successful bold plays can turn into iconic moments — the game-winning basket, the dunk that silenced the arena — and iconic moments earn legacy points in the final verdict.

4. Playoffs, awards and longevity

Once you reach the playoffs, series are decided in pivotal games and the finals are a full game-by-game series. Over your career you rack up rings, MVPs, season awards, and records — and you age: your peak arrives around 26-29 years old, and after that your body starts to charge interest. Knowing when to hang it up is also part of your legacy.

5. The verdict

At the end, the game weighs everything — points, rings, MVPs, iconic moments, longevity — and stamps your tier: from Playground Baller through Role Player, Starter, All-Star, Superstar and Legend, up to the top. THE GOAT demands a career on the level of Jordan (5+ rings and 4+ MVPs) or LeBron (38,000+ points and 4+ rings), plus an elite legacy score. Very few careers get there.

Quick tips

  • A small malus on an attribute you don't use is almost free — read the price before you steal.
  • A rebuilding team gives you more minutes and stats; a contender gives you a better shot at a ring. The verdict values both.
  • The bold play is mathematically better than the reckless play in most cases — courage yes, recklessness rarely.
  • Iconic moments only come from successful non-safe plays. Play it safe every time and you'll never become a legend.
